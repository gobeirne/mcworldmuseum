Museum

Build a museum of your old worlds

Open your Bedrock worlds, choose the places you remember, and get back one new world with each of them as an exhibit, side by side.

  1. Open your worlds
  2. Keep the places you remember
  3. Arrange them in the museum
  4. Build it and play

Everything happens in this browser. Your worlds aren’t uploaded anywhere and the originals aren’t changed.

On iPhone: in Files, long-press a world folder, tap Compress, then choose the ZIP here. A ZIP of the whole minecraftWorlds folder works too. On a computer you can drop ZIPs or folders here.
